Ben Clarke Bespoke

Furniture with a sense of place.

Bespoke pieces, designed around the way you live and made with an appreciation for timber, proportion and detail.

Based in Poole, Dorset, Ben Clarke Bespoke creates handmade furniture and cabinetry for individual homes and considered spaces. From a single freestanding piece to a complete fitted installation, every project is approached as something unique.

What we do

Made around you.

We work with homeowners, designers and architects, from an initial idea or drawing through to the finished piece.

Freestanding furniture

Tables, storage, seating and one-off pieces designed and made specifically for your space.

Fitted cabinetry

Built-in furniture and cabinetry that makes the most of a room while feeling integral to its architecture.

Unusual commissions

Have an idea that doesn't fit a standard brief? We enjoy the unusual. Bring us the problem, the sketch or simply the idea.

A considered approach

From a drawing, a conversation or a blank sheet of paper.

We can work from your plans, collaborate with a designer or help develop the piece from the very beginning. The aim is simple: thoughtful furniture that feels right in its setting.
